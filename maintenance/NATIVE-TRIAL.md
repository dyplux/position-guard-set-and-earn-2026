# Finite native protection trial preparation

`native_trial.cjs` prepares unsigned transactions for one F9 trial on chain 97. It has no signer, RPC writer, execution CLI or grant loader. Global financial flags remain false. Existing maintenance, Core reader, canonical receipts and historical policy remain unchanged.

The fixed five-day `NativeOnlyTestProfileV1` bounds one seed of 0.002 tBNB, one borrow of 0.0005 tBNB, an optional measured partial repayment, one native close with at most 0.0006 tBNB outer value and a redeemUnderlying no larger than the original seed. Each proposed transaction has at most 1,000,000 gas at 0.2 gwei. Actual aggregate fees can't exceed 0.001 tBNB; a 0.01 tBNB reserve and remaining gas budget must remain available. An unsigned profile doesn't authorize payment.

Before any proposal, the input needs complete fresh observations of all 49 registered Core markets, VAI zero, only native collateral entered, exact current accrued debt, exchange rate, balances, source bindings, native factors and equal positive native collateral/debt prices. Missing/unknown values stop. Monetary inputs are canonical decimal strings. No cross-token debts are added. The profile's same-asset test assumption doesn't establish oracle freshness. The planner uses the source's integer truncation order for collateral/debt capacity, with a conservative hard floor of 2 on both CF and LT ratios and protection target liquidation HF 4.

Day 1 starts with the first verified seed receipt's UTC timestamp. Borrow must share that UTC date. Day 2 partial repayment waits at least 24 hours and a later UTC date, and pays only the measured amount needed to reach target HF 4. It returns NO_ACTION if the target already holds. Day 3 close/redeem wait at least 48 hours and the third UTC date. History needs distinct, confirmed, quorum-verified canonical receipts, ordered steps and measured fees/effects. The module validates their normalized fields; it doesn't implement an independent receipt verifier or live source-binding collector. Those adapters, exact latest two-RPC simulation and independent review remain execution gates.

Maximillion is fixed to the official native adapter `0xF3a34e06015e019D6154a0f1089f695B27122f50`, its complete runtime pin and its `vBnb()` getter. `repayBehalf(F9)` reads accrued debt inside the transaction, repays the lesser of input/debt and refunds surplus to F9. It counts as one outer economic transaction. A direct `repayBorrow()` quote is also available as a codec with no full-close guarantee: new interest can leave dust, and overpayment reverts. Redeem remains blocked until a fresh complete observation proves zero native debt. A proposed close never claims success before canonical receipt and zero-debt effects.
